ABOUT DIMEDICE

Dice rolling,
without the clutter.

DimeDice is a simple, customizable dice roller designed to make rolling dice feel tactile, clear, and satisfying.

01

What is DimeDice?

DimeDice lets you roll multiple dice, choose different dice types, and keep track of your recent rolls. Everything is designed around a focused and distraction-free rolling experience.

02

Features

01

Multiple Dice

Roll between one and six dice at a time.

02

Multiple Dice Types

Choose from D6, D8, D10, D12, and D20.

03

Roll History

Keep track of your most recent rolls and results.

04

Keyboard Friendly

Press Space to roll without reaching for the mouse.

03

Privacy & Data

DimeDice does not require an account. Your dice preferences and roll history are stored locally in your browser using local storage.

This means your saved data stays on your device and is not stored on a DimeDice server.

04
